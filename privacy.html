Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This policy describes how Task Master (“the app”) handles information when you use its deal and refund tracking features. Keep this policy aligned with the app's actual behavior.

Important: Task Master is designed to keep tracking records on the user's device. If you enable Google Drive backup, the app may access the Drive files it creates for backup and restore after you grant permission.

Information stored in the app

The app may store order, mediator, refund, date, and related tracking information you enter. In local-only mode, this information is stored in the app's local database on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove local records, so keep a separate backup.

Google Drive backup

If you choose Google Drive backup, the app requests the Drive permission shown on the Google consent screen. It uses that permission to upload, list, download, or update backup files for backup and restore. Backup contents may include tracking information you chose to save. Backups are stored in your Google Drive account and are subject to Google's terms and privacy practices.

You can decline Google Drive authorization and continue using features that do not require Drive access. You can revoke access from your Google Account security settings. Revoking access may stop future backups and restores until permission is granted again.

Use and sharing

Information entered into the app is used to provide tracking, backup, and restore features. Do not enter sensitive information that is not needed for tracking. Confirm the app's implementation before making claims about analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party services.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Drive backups remain in Google Drive until you delete them or they are otherwise removed. Deleting a local record does not necessarily delete a previously created Drive backup.

Security

Use a device lock and protect your Google account. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. Update the effective date after material revisions.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page. Replace the placeholder contact details there with a support email you control before publishing.
